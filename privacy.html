Privacy Policy

Balcony Bloom · Last updated October 22, 2026

Balcony Bloom does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What stays on your device

Your balcony, coins, plant album, guest book and settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to the developer or anyone else.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example, when a plant is ready to harvest). These are local notifications; no data leaves your device.

No tracking, no ads, no accounts

The game has no ads, no analytics, no third-party software development kits and no sign-in. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Because no personal data is collected, the game is safe to play at any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
