For iPhone and iPad

A cozy balcony garden that grows while you're away.

Plant herbs, flowers and fruit, decorate your balcony, follow the seasons and welcome feathered and furry visitors. A little calm for every day.

Coming soon on theApp Store Free · No ads · No account
  1. 1

    Plant and water

    Drag a plant onto your balcony, water it when it's thirsty and harvest it when it's ripe.

  2. 2

    Decorate

    Add sofas, lanterns, fountains and new floors. A cozy balcony earns more and attracts more guests.

  3. 3

    Come back later

    Your garden keeps growing while the app is closed. A forgotten plant never dies; it just waits for water.

Small balcony, lots to grow

Everything you need for a calm daily ritual, and nothing that rushes you.

Grows in real time

One game day lasts one real hour. Short, calm visits fit into your day, and something is always ready when you return.

Four seasons and weather

Spring rain waters your plants, summer sun ripens tomatoes, autumn leaves drift by and winter covers the railing in snow.

Decorate your way

Place furniture and decor on a tile grid, then switch to a wooden deck, marble or mosaic floor and a new railing.

Meet your visitors

Butterflies, bees, an owl, a squirrel and the neighbor's cats drop by. Figure out what each one likes, and one cat might move in.

Collect them all

Fill your plant album with growth stages and harvest stamps, and complete it to unlock a special lemon tree.

Build your tower

Save up coins to add new floors, each with its own balcony shape, and watch your building change with the seasons.

A caretaker for busy days

Hire a gardener to water thirsty plants and harvest ripe ones on every floor, for a small fee. Available every four hours.

Saved games

Save your garden under a name and come back to it any time. Keep up to ten saved games.

Syncs between devices

Turn on iCloud sync and your garden follows you between your iPhone and iPad. It's optional and stays in your own Apple Account.

Made for iPhone and iPad

One free download for both. On iPad the balcony gets more room and shows more of the neighborhood.

Ready for iPhone Duo

The balcony stays centered on every screen, and the garden runs edge to edge around the status bar.
