Common questions

Do my plants die if I forget them?
No. A plant that runs out of water wilts and stops growing, then recovers after you water it.

Do plants grow while the app is closed?
Yes, for up to one real day. A game day lasts one real hour, and a season lasts three hours.

Where is my progress saved?
On your device only. Deleting the app deletes your balcony.

How do I change the language, music or sound?
Tap the gear button in the top right of the game.

How do I see the tutorial again?
Settings, then Replay tutorial.
